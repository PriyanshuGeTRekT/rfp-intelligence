# Bid comparison: Bid1, Bid2, Bid3

Bid1 (JA-207652) targets a large volume of student and staff devices with a longer 3-year term and cooperative procurement; Bid2 (#E20P4600040) is a fixed-price offer under a state master contract with a short 12-month term; Bid3 (2025-003) has the latest deadline and a bid security requirement but lacks many product details. The main distinctions lie in due dates, submission methods, contractual terms, required bond, payment, and delivery schedules, as well as the specific device offerings and documentation needs. These differences should guide which bid aligns best with procurement timing, financial terms, and device requirements.

## Key differences

- **Due Date**: Bid1 due date is July 9, 2024; Bid2 is June 10, 2024, earlier; Bid3 is September 6, 2024, latest.
- **Pre Bid Meeting**: Bid1 has a pre-bid meeting on June 10, 2024; Bid2 and Bid3 do not specify a pre-bid meeting.
- **Bid Submission Type**: Bid1 allows electronic submission via iSupplier portal plus manual sealed envelope; Bid2 requires submission only through eMMA; Bid3 mandates sealed envelope by September 6.
- **Term of Bid**: Bid1 offers a 3-year term with two one-year renewals up to 5 years; Bid2 provides fixed price with 90-day validity; Bid3 offers a 12-month term.
- **Bid Bond Requirement**: Bid3 requires a 10% bid security in cash or a bid bond; Bid1 and Bid2 have no specified bid bond requirement.
- **Payment Terms**: Bid1 requires payment within 10 days of receiving payment from the district; Bid2 requires invoices within 10 days of delivery; Bid3 has no payment terms stated.
- **Contract or Cooperative to use**: Bid1 is under EPCNT/CTPA cooperatives; Bid2 is under the Desktop, Laptop and Tablet Master Contract; Bid3 has no cooperative listed.
- **Any Additional Documentation Required**: Bid1 requires multiple forms including INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT and certificates; Bid2 requires Mercury Affidavit and Contract Affidavit; Bid3 has no additional documentation specified.
- **Product**: Bid1 requests 150,000 devices (50,000 each of three types); Bid2 requests 30 Dell Latitude laptops and 30 docks; Bid3 has no product listed.
- **Model_no**: Bid1 has no model specified; Bid2 specifies CC7802 and WD22TB4; Bid3 specifies Dell Chrome 3120 Chromebook.
- **Part_no**: Bid2 lists numerous part numbers; Bid1 and Bid3 have none.
- **MFG for Registration**: Bid1 OEM; Bid2 Dell; Bid3 Dell.
- **Product Specification**: Bid1 lists Windows devices with specific specs; Bid2 specifies Intel Core Ultra 5 and related specs; Bid3 lists HP Fortis 11-inch Chromebook specs.
- **company_name**: Bid1 Dallas Independent School District; Bid2 State of Maryland Treasurer's Office; Bid3 Delano Union School District.
- **contact_info**: Bid1 provides procurement contact details; Bid2 provides contact details; Bid3 has no contact info.
- **Installation**: Bid1 requires white glove deployment services; Bid2 requires all deliveries and deployment; Bid3 has no installation requirement listed.
- **Delivery Date**: Bid1 has no specified delivery date; Bid2 requires delivery within 45 days of award; Bid3 specifies delivery on October 31, 2024.
