# Bid comparison: Bid1, Bid2, Bid3

The bids differ across key procurement parameters such as submission methods, deadlines, contractual terms, bond requirements, product specifications, and delivery schedules. Bid1 targets a large quantity of tablets with a flexible 3‑year term and white‑glove deployment, Bid2 focuses on a small quantity of Dell laptops under a fixed‑price, short‑term contract, and Bid3 offers a significant fleet of Chromebooks with a 12‑month term and a stricter bid bond. Decision‑makers should weigh the urgency of delivery, the need for white‑glove services, and the financial security provisions before choosing the most suitable bid.

## Key differences

- **Bid Number**: The bids have distinct identifiers: JA-207652 (Bid1), BPM044557 (Bid2), and 2025-003 (Bid3).
- **Title**: The titles differ: Bid1 is 'Student and Staff Computing Devices', Bid2 is 'Dell Laptops w/Extended Warranty', and Bid3 has no title provided.
- **Due Date**: Bid2 has the earliest due date of 2024-06-10 14:00 EDT, Bid1 follows at 2024-07-09 14:00 CDT, and Bid3 is the latest at 2024-09-06 13:00.
- **Pre Bid Meeting**: Bid1 specifies a pre‑bid meeting on 2024-06-10 14:00 CST via TEAMS, whereas Bid2 and Bid3 do not provide a pre‑bid meeting schedule.
- **Bid Submission Type**: Bid1 requires online submission via the iSupplier portal or a sealed hard copy, Bid2 mandates electronic submission through the eMMA portal only, and Bid3 provides no submission method.
- **Term of Bid**: Bid1 offers a 3‑year initial term with up to two one‑year extensions (maximum five years), Bid2 has a fixed price term valid for thirty days, and Bid3 has a twelve‑month term.
- **Bid Bond Requirement**: Bid1 does not state a bid bond requirement, Bid2 also does not state one, but Bid3 requires a 10% bid security in cash, a certified cashier’s check, or a bid bond.
- **Payment Terms**: Bid1 stipulates payment within ten days of receiving payment from the district, Bid2 requires invoices emailed and submitted within ten days of equipment delivery, and Bid3 has no payment terms stated.
- **Contract or Cooperative to use**: Bid1 references the Educational Purchasing Cooperative of North Texas and the Central Texas Purchasing Alliance, Bid2 references the Master Contract: Desktop, Laptop and Tablet 2015 Master Contract, and Bid3 refers to a Purchase Contract.
- **Any Additional Documentation Required**: Bid1 lists INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT CONSENT FORM, FORM 1295, and other documents; Bid2 lists MERCURY CONTENT INFORMATION and a Proposal Affidavit; Bid3 lists Instructions to Bidders, Bid Form, Substitution Listing, Workers' Compensation Certificate, Guarantee, and a required signed form.
- **Product**: Bid1 proposes 50,000 Student Tablets, Bid2 proposes 30 Dell Latitude 5550 laptops and 30 Dell Thunderbolt 4 Docks, and Bid3 proposes 500 Dell Chrome 3120 Chromebooks plus a Google Chrome OS Management Console license.
- **Model_no**: Bid1 has no model numbers listed, Bid2 lists Dell Latitude 5550 and Dell Thunderbolt 4 Dock – WD22TB4, and Bid3 lists Dell Chrome 3120 Chromebook and a Google Chrome OS Management Console license and Dell Optical Mouse.
- **Part_no**: Bid1 does not provide part numbers, Bid2 lists multiple Dell part numbers such as 210-BLYZ and others, and Bid3 does not list part numbers.
- **MFG for Registration**: Bid1 specifies OEM for registration, Bid2 indicates Dell, and Bid3 specifies Dell Chrome 3120 Chromebook or equivalent.
- **Product Specification**: Bid1 details Student Windows specs including 1920-1080 resolution, 16GB DDR5, 256GB NVMe SSD; Bid2 provides Intel Core Ultra 5 125U processor, 16GB DDR5 at 5600 MT/s; Bid3 specifies HP Fortis 11 inch G10 Chromebook specs and Intel Processor N100.
- **company_name**: Bid1 is from Dallas Independent School District, Bid2 from the State of Maryland Treasurer's Office, and Bid3 from Delano Union School District.
- **contact_info**: Bid1 contacts Jasmine Alzate (buyer) at JALZATE@dallasisd.org, Bid2 contacts Tamaira Hawkins at thawkins@treasurer.state.md.us, and Bid3 contacts Sandra Rivera at srivera@duesd.org.
- **Installation**: Bid1 requires white glove deployment services; Bid2 and Bid3 do not specify installation requirements.
- **Delivery Date**: Bid1 does not specify a delivery date, Bid2 requires delivery within 45 days of award, and Bid3 requires delivery by November 30, 2024.
