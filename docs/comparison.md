# Bid comparison: Bid1, Bid2, Bid3

Bid2 has the earliest deadline and the most detailed product specifications, making it the most attractive for timely procurement. It also imposes strict submission and contract requirements, but offers a fixed-price, 90‑day validity term, which could simplify budgeting. Bid1 offers a longer term with broader cooperative agreements and white‑glove deployment, but lacks a clear delivery date and has no bond requirement. Bid3 requires a bid bond, provides a single product model, and sets a firm delivery date, but offers the shortest term and minimal documentation.

## Key differences

- **Bid Number**: Bid1 uses JA-207652, Bid2 uses #E20P4600040, and Bid3 uses 2025-003.
- **Title**: Bid1's title is 'Student and Staff Computing Devices'; Bid2's is 'Dell Laptops w/Extended Warranty'; Bid3 has no title.
- **Due Date**: Bid2's due date (2024-06-10 14:00 EDT) is earlier than Bid1 (2024-07-09 14:00 CDT) and Bid3 (2024-09-06 13:00).
- **Pre Bid Meeting**: Only Bid1 offers a pre-bid meeting scheduled for 2024-06-10 14:00 CDT; Bid2 and Bid3 have none.
- **Bid Submission Type**: Bid1 allows electronic submission via iSupplier portal plus a manual sealed hard copy; Bid2 requires submission exclusively through eMMA; Bid3 requires proposals in a sealed envelope by 1:00 PM on September 6, 2024.
- **Term of Bid**: Bid1 offers a three-year initial term with two one-year renewals up to five years; Bid2 is a fixed-price bid valid for at least 90 days after the due date; Bid3 offers a twelve-month term.
- **Bid Bond Requirement**: Only Bid3 requires a 10% bid security in cash, cashier’s check, or bid bond; Bid1 and Bid2 have no bond requirement.
- **Payment Terms**: Bid2 mandates invoices emailed to the specified address and submitted within 10 days of delivery; Bid1 and Bid3 have no payment terms listed.
- **Contract or Cooperative to use**: Bid1 involves the EPCNT Interlocal Agreement Rider and the CTPA Interlocal Agreement; Bid2 references the Desktop, Laptop and Tablet 2015 Master Contract, 060B5400007; Bid3 has no contract information.
- **Any Additional Documentation Required**: Bid1 requires INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT CONSENT FORM, MWBE Forms, W9, optional Punch Out Process, 1295 form, and optional non-warranty options; Bid2 requires Mercury Affidavit and Contract Affidavit; Bid3 has no documentation requirements listed.
- **Product**: Bid2 specifies 30 Dell Latitude 5550 laptops and 30 Dell Thunderbolt 4 Dock – WD22TB4; Bid1 and Bid3 have no product listed.
- **Model_no**: Bid2 lists model numbers CC7802 and WD22TB4; Bid3 lists Dell Chrome 3120 Chromebook; Bid1 lists no model numbers.
- **Part_no**: Bid2 lists multiple part numbers (210-BLYZ, 379-BFNZ, etc.); Bid1 and Bid3 list none.
- **MFG for Registration**: Bid1 uses OEM for registration; Bid2 and Bid3 use Dell.
- **Product Specification**: Bid1 specifies Intel i5 14th Gen CPUs, 16GB DDR5 RAM, 512GB NVMe SSD, 13” display, and 3-year warranty; Bid2 specifies Intel Core Ultra 5 125U, 16GB DDR5, etc.; Bid3 specifies HP Fortis 11-inch G10 Chromebook with Intel N100 3.40 GHz.
- **company_name**: Bid1 is from Dallas Independent School District; Bid2 from State of Maryland Treasurer's Office; Bid3 from Delano Union School District.
- **contact_info**: Bid1 contact is Procurement Services at 972-925-3700, ProcurementCS@dallasisd.org; Bid2 contact is Tamaira Hawkins at 410-260-7533, thawkins@treasurer.state.md.us; Bid3 has no contact information.
- **Installation**: Bid1 requires white glove deployment services, asset detailing, reporting, etching, and delivery; Bid2 and Bid3 have no installation requirements listed.
- **Delivery Date**: Bid2 requires delivery within 45 days of award; Bid3 specifies delivery by October 31, 2024; Bid1 has no delivery date listed.
