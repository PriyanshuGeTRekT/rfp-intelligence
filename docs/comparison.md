# Bid comparison: Bid1, Bid2, Bid3

The three bids differ in several key aspects. Bid1 has a later due date, requires both electronic and manual submission, offers a three‑year term with renewal options, and includes deployment services and interlocal agreements; it also has no bid bond or payment terms listed. Bid2 requires a 90‑day validity, uses a fixed‑price e‑procurement system, mandates a master contract, and requires invoices within 10 days of delivery, but does not require a bond. Bid3 demands a 10% bid bond, specifies a delivery date of 31 Oct 2024, and has no term or payment terms listed. Product specifications vary: Bid2 supplies Dell Latitude laptops with docks, Bid3 specifies a Chromebook, while Bid1’s product details are not disclosed. Overall, the choice depends on preferred contract length, submission method, and product requirements.

## Key differences

- **Bid Number**: Bid1 uses JA-207652, Bid2 uses #E20P4600040, Bid3 uses 2025-003.
- **Title**: Bid1 title is 'Student and Staff Computing Devices', Bid2 'Dell Laptops w/Extended Warranty', Bid3 has no title.
- **Due Date**: Bid1 due 2024-07-09 14:00 CDT, Bid2 2024-06-10 14:00 EDT, Bid3 2024-09-06 13:00.
- **Pre Bid Meeting**: Bid1 has pre-bid meeting on 2024-06-10 14:00 CDT, Bid2 and Bid3 have none.
- **Bid Submission Type**: Bid1 accepts electronic via iSupplier portal plus manual hard copy; Bid2 accepts electronic via eMMA only; Bid3 requires sealed envelope delivered by September 6 1pm.
- **Term of Bid**: Bid1 has three‑year initial term with two one‑year renewals up to five years; Bid2 fixed price valid 90 days; Bid3 twelve months.
- **Bid Bond Requirement**: Bid1 and Bid2 have none, Bid3 requires 10% bid security.
- **Payment Terms**: Bid1 none, Bid2 invoices within 10 days of delivery, Bid3 none.
- **Contract or Cooperative to use**: Bid1 uses Educational Purchasing Cooperative of North Texas and Central Texas Purchasing Alliance; Bid2 uses Desktop, Laptop and Tablet 2015 Master Contract 060B5400007; Bid3 none.
- **Any Additional Documentation Required**: Bid1 requires INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT, MWBE, W9, Punch Out optional, 1295, Non-warranty options; Bid2 none; Bid3 none.
- **Product**: Bid1 product not found; Bid2 Dell Latitude 5550 with dock; Bid3 product not found.
- **Model_no**: Bid1 not found; Bid2 Dell Latitude 5550 and WD22TB4; Bid3 Dell Chrome 3120 Chromebook.
- **Part_no**: Bid1 not found; Bid2 list of 13 part numbers; Bid3 not found.
- **MFG for Registration**: Bid1 OEM, Bid2 Dell, Bid3 Dell.
- **Product Specification**: Bid1 spec: Intel i5 14th Gen, 16GB DDR5, 512GB NVMe, 13+ inch, 3‑year warranty; Bid2 spec: Intel Core Ultra 5 125U, 16GB DDR5 5600 MT/s; Bid3 spec: HP Fortis 11 inch G10 Chromebook, 1366x768, N100 3.4 GHz.
- **company_name**: Bid1 Dallas Independent School District; Bid2 State of Maryland Treasurer's Office; Bid3 Delano Union School District.
- **contact_info**: Bid1 contact Procurement Services; Bid2 Tamaira Hawkins; Bid3 none.
- **Installation**: Bid1 includes white glove deployment services; Bid2 none; Bid3 none.
- **Delivery Date**: Bid1 not found; Bid2 within 45 days of award; Bid3 October 31, 2024.
